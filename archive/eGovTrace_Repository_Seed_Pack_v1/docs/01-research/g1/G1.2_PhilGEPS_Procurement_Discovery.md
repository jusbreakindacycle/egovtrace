# G1.2 — PhilGEPS Procurement Discovery

**Program:** eGovTrace / Philippine State Control & Assurance Research  
**Research Track:** FMR Reconciliation Experiment  
**Gate:** G1.2  
**Status:** FROZEN  
**Disposition:** PARTIAL PASS

## 1. Purpose

Test whether the FMR Watch anchor can be discovered in the national public procurement environment through PhilGEPS and related official procurement surfaces.

## 2. Test dimensions

The search tested combinations of:

- contractor name
- project / FMR terminology
- New Casay / New Casayuran
- Braulio E. Dujali
- Davao del Norte
- FY2025 context
- reported project amount

The goal was not to force a match. False positives and near-matches were treated as first-class findings.

## 3. Findings

### 3.1 PhilGEPS public procurement surface — CONFIRMED

A public procurement search surface exists and is usable for procurement discovery.

### 3.2 Exact anchor procurement match — NOT ESTABLISHED

The initial search did not produce a confidently matching PhilGEPS procurement record for the FMR Watch anchor.

This does **not** establish that no procurement record exists.

It establishes that an exact public join was not demonstrated through the search performed.

### 3.3 Contractor search — PARTIAL / USEFUL

The anchor contractor name could be searched, and related historical-name evidence was useful for discovery. Contractor identity remains separate from project identity.

### 3.4 Municipality / location search — USABLE

Braulio E. Dujali and Davao del Norte produced procurement candidates and related records.

### 3.5 Financial amount search — NOT CONCLUSIVE

The reported ₱14.92 million contract amount did not independently establish a procurement match.

## 4. Core methodological finding

Procurement discovery can produce plausible candidates that must be rejected unless identity, location, contractor, timing, and transaction-specific evidence align.

Therefore:

> similarity is not identity.

## 5. Frozen disposition

**G1.2: PARTIAL PASS**

The procurement layer is publicly discoverable, but a direct PhilGEPS-to-FMR Watch anchor join was not established at this gate.

The appropriate next step was therefore expanded procurement testing rather than assuming the first plausible candidate was the target.

## 7. Evidence sources

- PhilGEPS public procurement search surface — https://philgeps.gov.ph/
- DA FMR Watch anchor and related official DA project reporting were used as the upstream identity reference.
