# G1.2a — Expanded Procurement Identity Test

**Program:** eGovTrace / Philippine State Control & Assurance Research  
**Research Track:** FMR Reconciliation Experiment  
**Gate:** G1.2a  
**Status:** FROZEN  
**Disposition:** PASS for procurement discovery; anchor reconciliation PARTIAL

## 1. Purpose

Expand the procurement search beyond the first PhilGEPS pass and test whether an official procurement candidate can be confidently joined to the FMR Watch anchor without relying on name similarity alone.

## 2. Significant discovery

A real DPWH procurement record was found:

**Contract ID:** `25LJ0147`  
**Project:** Concreting of Farm to Market Road, Purok 4, D-5, Barangay Dujali to Purok 3, Bugtong Talisay Barangay Dujali, Braulio E. Dujali, Davao del Norte

The procurement documentation indicated that the opportunity was posted on DPWH and PhilGEPS on February 27, 2025, with a supplemental bulletin dated March 11, 2025.

The contract agreement identifies:

**Agong Builders and Construction Supply, Inc.**

rather than the FMR Watch anchor contractor:

**Ruplino Seismundo Construction Corp.**

## 3. Identity challenge

The procurement candidate shares important contextual attributes with the anchor:

- same municipality
- same province
- FMR terminology
- comparable infrastructure domain
- 2025 timeframe

Nevertheless, the contractor and location details do not establish that it is the same intervention.

The candidate was therefore **not merged** with the anchor.

## 5. Research finding

The most valuable result is not merely that an official procurement was found.

It is that the research **rejected a plausible near-match**.

This validates the need for identity reconciliation using multiple attributes rather than fuzzy text matching.

## 6. Frozen disposition

**G1.2a: PASS for procurement discovery, PARTIAL for anchor reconciliation.**

The procurement layer can produce strong native identifiers, but those identifiers must not be attached to the FMR Watch intervention until the relationship is evidenced.
