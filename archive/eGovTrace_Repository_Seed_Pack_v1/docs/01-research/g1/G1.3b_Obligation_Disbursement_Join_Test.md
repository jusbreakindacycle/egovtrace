# G1.3b — Obligation & Disbursement Join Test

**Program:** eGovTrace / Philippine State Control & Assurance Research  
**Research Track:** FMR Reconciliation Experiment  
**Gate:** G1.3b  
**Status:** FROZEN  
**Disposition:** FAIL for direct public project-level join; PASS for agency-reporting observability

## 1. Purpose

Determine whether the New Casay FMR can be followed from allotment into specific obligation and disbursement amounts using public FAR / SAOB reporting.

## 2. Reporting infrastructure

DBM publicly maintains Statement of Appropriations, Allotments, Obligations, Disbursements and Balances (SAOB) reporting.

DA likewise publishes FAR No. 1 and related quarterly and monthly financial accountability reports.

Therefore the government clearly reports the financial lifecycle at institutional reporting level.

## 3. Obligation finding

The public FAR/SAOB structures expose obligation categories and quarterly reporting.

However, the research did not establish a reproducible New Casay-specific obligation row carrying an amount that can safely be associated with the FMR anchor or its known budget representations.

**Status: UNRESOLVED**

This is not evidence that no obligation exists.

## 4. Disbursement finding

DA publishes FAR No. 4 disbursement reporting and DBM publishes NCA utilization information.

However, no project-specific New Casay disbursement amount was independently recovered and joined to the anchor.

A broad NCA utilization amount cannot automatically be interpreted as payment for one FMR.

**Status: NOT ESTABLISHED**

## 5. The public lifecycle break

The observed structure is better represented as:

```text
REAL-WORLD FMR
   ├── FMR Watch
   ├── DA project report
   ├── DBM NEP
   ├── DBM GAA
   ├── DBM adjustment / release representation
   ├── FAR / SAOB obligations ?
   └── FAR / NCA disbursements ?
```

The break is not necessarily missing data. It is the absence of a demonstrated public project-level cross-stage join.

## 6. Methodological consequence

The experiment rejects the idea that public FAR tables should be forced to perform the role of transaction-level ledgers if their published structure was not designed for that purpose.

The next investigation must target native transaction and authority identifiers.

## 7. Scorecard

| Stage | Status |
|---|---|
| Financial lifecycle reporting exists | CONFIRMED |
| Project-level NEP | PASS |
| Project-level GAA | PASS |
| Project-level obligation join | UNRESOLVED |
| Project-level disbursement join | UNRESOLVED |
| Direct project → payment join | NOT ESTABLISHED |

## 8. Frozen disposition

**G1.3b: FAIL for direct public project-level obligation/disbursement joining, while PASSING the observability test for institutional financial reporting.**

This failure is a research result, not an allegation of missing money or misconduct.
