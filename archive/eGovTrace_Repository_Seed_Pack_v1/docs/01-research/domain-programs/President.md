My fellow Filipinos, if I am to lead this country as your President, I will not stand by and allow the hard-earned money of taxpayers to be swallowed by systemic corruption. For decades, infrastructure funding has been treated as a political cash cow, resulting in the tragic irony of multi-billion peso flood control structures that disappear under water—or worse, do not exist at all.
The recent findings are unacceptable: the Commission on Audit (COA) uncovering "ghost" flood control structures, and the Department of Agriculture (DA) under Secretary [Francisco Tiu Laurel Jr.](https://www.google.com/search?q=francisco+tiu+laurel+jr.&kgmid=/g/11l6c1ttck) exposing millions of pesos worth of "ghost" Farm-to-Market Roads (FMRs) across Mindanao and the Bicol region. The fact that funds are fully disbursed through falsified Statements of Work Accomplished (SWA) while our farmers suffer and our cities drown is nothing short of economic sabotage.
As your Chief Executive, my response will be swift, evidence-based, and ruthless against corruption. We will use the full force of the Department of Information and Communications Technology (DICT) and the state’s digital ecosystem to permanently end this culture of impunity.
------------------------------
## 🏛️ 1. Absolute Transparency: Launching "Project Victoria" (The Open-Data Infrastructure Registry)
We will leverage Executive Order No. 119 (Data and Cloud Sovereignty) and the expanded ₱18.9-B budget of the [DICT](https://dict.gov.ph/) to build a blockchain-backed, public-facing portal called Project Victoria.

* 
* Mandatory Digital Tagging: Every single project funded under the national budget—whether a DPWH floodway, a National Irrigation Administration (NIA) reservoir, or a DA Farm-to-Market Road—will be required to register a unique cryptographic ID.
* The eGov Chain Protocol: Utilizing the DICT's eGovChain ledger, all project milestones, procurement documents, and funding releases will be permanently recorded. Once a document like a Statement of Work Accomplished is uploaded, it cannot be retroactively altered, falsified, or hidden from public and audit scrutiny.
* 

## 📡 2. Ending "Ghost Projects" via Geospatial and Citizen Verification
We will no longer rely solely on paper certifications signed behind closed doors.

* 
* Satellite and LiDAR Validation: Through a partnership with Project NOAH (UP Resilience Institute) and the Philippine Space Agency (PhilSA), the DICT will integrate high-resolution temporal satellite imagery into our digital system. If a contractor claims an FMR in Davao Occidental or a flood wall along the Angat River is completed, the system will cross-reference historical satellite tracks. If the imagery shows zero ground breaking, funding releases are automatically frozen by the system.
* Citizen Auditing via eGov PH Super App: We will roll out a feature in the eGov PH Super App called "Sumbong sa Imprastraktura." Citizens can locate any government project on a map, view its allocated budget, and upload geo-tagged, time-stamped photos of the actual site. If a project is missing or substandard, the report bypasses local bureaucrats and goes straight to a centralized task force.
* 

## ⚖️ 3. Direct Institutional Punitive Action
Data is useless without consequences. I will issue an Executive Order enforcing immediate accountability structures:

* 
* The "One Basin, One Plan" Mandate: The DPWH will be legally barred from executing standalone flood control projects. Following systemic climate logic, all flood mitigation must align strictly with the customized master plans of the 18 Major River Basins, integrated dynamically with NIA's agricultural irrigation and local LGU drainage blueprints. No regional alignment, no budget allocation.
* Interoperable Blacklisting via PhilGEPS: The DICT will integrate the [Procurement Service-DBM Open Data Portal](https://ps-philgeps.gov.ph/home/index.php/about-ps/news/8025-ps-dbm-enhances-open-data-portal-to-boost-transparency-and-participation-in-public-procurement) with COA’s fraud databases. Any contractor caught abandoning a site or fabricating project completions—such as those exposed in recent fraud audits—will be automatically blacklisted across all government agencies simultaneously. They cannot escape a DA blacklist to secure a DPWH project the next day.
* Prosecution for Economic Sabotage: I will instruct the Department of Justice (DOJ) to work alongside the Office of the Ombudsman using the evidence compiled by DA's Internal Audit Service and COA's Performance Audit Office. Public officials and private contractors who conspire to siphon off multi-million peso infrastructure funds will be charged with Economic Sabotage, which carries the heaviest penalties under our laws.
