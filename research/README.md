# eGovTrace Research

This directory is the research index for eGovTrace.

## Purpose

Research supports engineering decisions, validates assumptions, and records empirical findings. Research does not automatically become product behavior.

## Current research families

- State accountability and operational control
- Source-system and data-boundary research
- Identity interoperability and cross-system continuity
- Project, planning, budget, procurement, financial, implementation, audit, and outcome tracing
- FMR / DPWH reconciliation experiments
- Public accountability and citizen-facing architecture
- Completeness and gap audit

## Evidence rules

- Connection is not corruption.
- Not observed is not absent.
- Unavailable is not false.
- Identifier recovery is not identifier proof.
- AI output is not a fact or legal determination.
- Material claims require provenance.
- Historical state must remain reproducible.

See `docs/research/README.md` for the bridge from research artifacts to implementation context.
