# Future Physical-Resilience Architecture Backlog

**Program:** eGovTrace / Philippine State Control & Assurance Research  
**Branch:** President-2 + President-2.2 Architecture Research  
**Status:** APPROVED RESEARCH DECISION RECORD  
**Purpose:** Preserve useful architectural discoveries from the national flood, mobility, spatial, resilience, and physical-systems research branch without contaminating the frozen FMR experimental ontology.

---

## 1. Decision

The President-2 and President-2.2 research branch is **accepted as a high-value future architecture input**, but it does **not** reopen or modify the frozen G0, G1, or G2 artifacts.

The branch is classified into four kinds of disposition:

```text
ABSORB
The concept is already represented adequately in the current model.
No ontology change is required.

DEFER
The concept is genuinely useful or new, but belongs to a future layer
and should not enter the current FMR experimental model.

REJECT
The proposed form is too broad, methodologically unsafe, or architecturally
misleading. The underlying objective may survive in another form.

VERIFY
The concept may be valuable, but its factual, legal, technical, or
operational assumptions must be independently established before adoption.
```

The controlling principle remains:

> **Do not expand the accountability/reconciliation ontology merely because a concept would be useful to a future national physical-resilience architecture.**

G0's ontology-expansion rule requires an actual experimental need, material reconciliation impact, explanatory improvement, and a concept that can be defined without importing unsupported assumptions.

## 2. Current Architecture Boundary

The frozen FMR model currently establishes five interconnected State-Universe areas:

```text
AUTHORITY
GEOGRAPHY
MONEY
ASSET / INFRASTRUCTURE
EVIDENCE / EVENT
```

with the cross-cutting dimensions:

```text
TIME
IDENTITY
AUTHORIZATION
PROVENANCE
```

The FMR ontology already represents project identity, project representations, institutions and roles, locations, physical road segments, funding, allocation, obligations, procurement, contracts, payments, lifecycle states/events, inspection, completion/acceptance, source records, evidence, assertions, inferences, contradictions, expected/missing observations, findings, and identity/temporal/provenance metadata.

G1 then established the practical access state of the FMR-specific source landscape, including explicit `AVAILABLE`, `PARTIALLY_AVAILABLE`, `UNRESOLVED`, and related states.

G2 deliberately narrowed the State Universe to only what the FMR experiment needs and explicitly rejected rebuilding a comprehensive national registry.

Therefore this backlog is **not a replacement ontology**. It is a future architecture holding area.

# 4. ABSORB — Already Represented

## 4.1 State-as-Graph

The President branch proposes an integrated national state graph in which agencies, programs, budgets, projects and physical systems are linked.

This is not a new foundation for eGovTrace. The existing model already treats institutional participation, authority, money, projects, infrastructure, evidence, and time as interconnected representations.

**Decision:** ABSORB conceptually; no ontology change.

---

## 4.2 Temporal State

President-2 explicitly proposes time-aware states such as:

```text
planned
→ appropriated
→ procured
→ awarded
→ started
→ delayed
→ completed
→ inspected
→ accepted
→ operational
→ maintained
```

This reinforces the existing temporal and lifecycle architecture rather than extending it.

**Decision:** ABSORB.

---

## 4.3 Evidence / Fact / Claim / Inference / Finding

The branch preserves the distinction:

```text
FACT
CLAIM
INFERENCE
FINDING
```

and explicitly retains the principle that a relationship or anomaly is not automatically corruption.

The FMR ontology already has assertions, interpretations/inferences, contradictions, findings, evidence artifacts, provenance, and expected/missing observations.

**Decision:** ABSORB.

---

## 4.4 Human Decision Authority

The branch states that AI should provide predictions, evidence, alternatives, confidence and uncertainty rather than automatic decrees.

The existing authority/access model already distinguishes:

```text
DATA CUSTODY
TECHNICAL ACCESS
OPERATIONAL AUTHORITY
INVESTIGATIVE AUTHORITY
DECISION AUTHORITY
```

Therefore this is reinforcement, not a missing G0 concept.

**Decision:** ABSORB.

---

## 4.5 Lifecycle Budgeting and O&M

The principle that a project must have a responsible operation-and-maintenance owner is compatible with the existing lifecycle and obligation framework.

It does not require a new ontology entity merely because the eventual lifecycle extends beyond construction.

The future system may add richer operational-state representations later.

**Decision:** ABSORB at the conceptual level; DEFER richer operational implementation.

# 5. DEFER — Genuine Future Architecture Concepts

## 5.1 Infrastructure Dependency

This is the strongest genuinely new architectural concept in the branch.

Examples:

```text
pump DEPENDS_ON electricity
hospital DEPENDS_ON road
port DEPENDS_ON logistics corridor
community DEPENDS_ON evacuation route
telecom DEPENDS_ON power
```

The dependency concept enables cascading-failure analysis.

It is not currently a first-class G0 relation.

**Decision:** DEFER.

**Future candidate relationship:**

```text
DEPENDS_ON
```

Before adoption into a formal ontology, the concept must receive its own adversarial challenge covering directionality, temporal validity, strength, conditionality, redundancy and evidence.

## 6.2 Environmental Hard Constraints

The branch proposes RED / YELLOW / GREEN ecological classification and a principle that some ecosystem losses cannot be compensated by economic benefits.

**Decision:** VERIFY.

Questions requiring formal validation include:

```text
Which constraints are legally absolute?
Which are policy constraints?
Which are engineering thresholds?
Who is the decision authority?
What is the appeal/review mechanism?
What evidence is required?
```

---

## 6.3 Automatic Funding Freeze

The branch contemplates physical or model-based signals producing funding holds.

The underlying assurance goal may be valid, but the automatic legal consequence cannot be assumed.

The safer architecture is:

```text
OBSERVATION
→ EVIDENCE
→ SIGNAL
→ AUTHORIZED REVIEW
→ DECISION
→ ACTION
→ AUDIT TRAIL
```

**Decision:** VERIFY.

Legal authority, due process, false-positive handling, appeal, delegated authority, and evidentiary standards must be established before any implementation proposal.

# 7. REJECT — Unsafe or Misleading Forms

## 7.1 Automatic Contractor Blacklisting from Graph Signals

The branch proposes rapid cross-government consequences when contractor irregularity is detected.

The analytical objective may be useful, but a graph relationship, pattern, or AI signal is not itself a legal finding.

The architecture must retain:

```text
RELATIONSHIP
≠
ALLEGATION
≠
FINDING
≠
LEGAL STATUS
```

Therefore automatic blacklisting directly from a graph signal is rejected.

A future compliant sequence would be:

```text
ANALYTICAL SIGNAL
→ EVIDENCE REVIEW
→ AUTHORIZED INVESTIGATION / PROCESS
→ FORMAL DECISION
→ PROCUREMENT STATUS CHANGE
```

**Decision:** REJECT as proposed; preserve the underlying accountability objective in a lawful review architecture.

---

## 7.2 AI as Automatic Government Authority

Both President files also contain the better principle that AI should not be the final authority.

Therefore any architecture in which an AI model itself issues legally consequential government decisions is rejected.

**Decision:** REJECT as a control pattern.

The supported model is:

```text
AI / MODEL
→ recommendation + evidence + uncertainty
→ responsible institution
→ lawful decision
```

---

## 7.3 Single Composite Project Score as the Authoritative Optimizer

The branch proposes a score combining economic value, social benefit, risk reduction, mobility, ecological damage, climate vulnerability and lifecycle cost.

The conceptual objective is sound, but a single scalar can conceal hard constraints and make incomparable policy dimensions appear commensurable.

**Decision:** REJECT as the authoritative current form.

The future architecture should preserve multidimensional evidence and constraints first. Composite scoring may be introduced later for specific decisions after validation.

# 9. What This Branch Does to FMR Specifically

The branch increases the eventual value of the FMR pilot without changing its present boundary.

Today the FMR experiment asks, in essence:

```text
Does an FMR intervention reconcile across:

authority
geography
money
procurement
contract
obligation
payment
physical evidence
```

The future physical-resilience system could eventually ask:

```text
Does this road:

connect people and farms effectively?
fit its watershed?
create or reduce flood risk?
connect to logistics?
remain operable under hazard?
depend on vulnerable infrastructure?
produce measurable economic benefit?
maintain required service over time?
```

Those are later questions.

The current FMR experiment is valuable precisely because it tests the lower-level evidence/reconciliation substrate needed before those higher-level questions can be answered reliably.

# 12. Expansion Trigger

A deferred concept may return to formal architecture only when one of the following occurs:

1. the FMR experiment encounters a real record or question that cannot be represented adequately;
2. the future physical-resilience program is separately approved as a research program;
3. independent domain evidence establishes the concept's legal/technical basis;
4. a formal adversarial challenge shows that the concept can be defined without unsafe assumptions;
5. the proposed concept has a clearly defined owner, authority, evidence basis, temporal behavior, and failure semantics where applicable.

A concept does not enter G0 merely because it appears repeatedly in policy research.
