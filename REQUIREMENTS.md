# Requirements

## T001
Initialize a runnable AI-native engineering repository.

## First prototype acceptance target
A reviewer can open a synthetic project, inspect expected and observed lifecycle events, relationships, reconciliation result, explainable control signal, supporting evidence, missing information, human verification, accountability action, outcome and public projection.

## Non-goals
No production government integration, autonomous case assignment, legal findings, corruption classification, universal source database, or unrestricted public exposure.
